The Work, Closer Up

01

Cloudshield

Security Operations Platform · Client Project

Cloudshield is organised around three movements: prevent, detect and respond. It watches a cloud estate continuously, reduces its posture to one honest headline figure, surfaces the single incident that matters before the ninety that do not, and explains the reasoning behind every verdict rather than asking an analyst to trust it blindly.

  • Behavioural baselining paired with a graph threat engine for anomaly detection
  • Each incident mapped to MITRE ATT&CK with a confidence score and annual risk figure
  • Automated containment gated by a safety check and a fail closed policy engine
  • Attack map built on offline geographic lookup, free of external dependency
  • Eighty passing automated tests and seven reproducible controlled experiments
  • Python
  • Graph neural networks
  • Federated learning
  • MITRE ATT&CK
  • Explainable AI
  • AWS
02

Swadeshi Distribution Platform

Sales Force Automation · Client Project

A distribution business runs on information usually scattered across paper, memory and phone calls. This platform gathers it into one operational view: every representative, every outlet and every route across the Kalawana territory. Delivered across five two week sprints, where I served first as Startup Manager and later as Risk Manager.

  • Built the returns, representative visit and invoice cancellation modules
  • Live sales trajectory, stock movement and top product reporting
  • Traced and resolved silent data loss caused by ephemeral container storage
  • Role based administrative access backed by a complete audit trail
  • Owned the risk plan, responsibility matrix and delivery documentation
  • Laravel 11
  • PHP
  • MySQL
  • REST API
  • Agile and Scrum
03

Smart Student Portal

Offline Capable Education System · Client Project

The Western Province Smart Student Portal was shaped by one demanding condition: the institution could not depend on the internet, so the system had to work without it. The result follows a student from sitting an examination to uploading a final assessment, and keeps that journey intact whether or not the network is there.

  • Registration, examinations and wellness checks all run without a connection
  • Uploads, payments and email stay online and reconcile the moment it returns
  • Funnel view exposing exactly where students fall away between stages
  • Payment verification queue matched on counter and bank reference
  • Offline capable
  • Progressive web app
  • Data sync
  • Dashboard design
04

Field Rep App

Android Companion · Mobile

A representative on a route has one hand free, a few seconds between customers and a connection that comes and goes. The companion app is built for exactly those conditions: orders, returns and visit confirmations captured at the counter, queued locally, and settled the moment signal returns.

  • Orders grouped by month with running totals and live status on every line
  • Cancellation handled in a single tap, with the reason recorded against the order
  • Routes, returns and stock reachable from one bar at the base of the screen
  • High contrast interface that stays legible in direct sunlight
  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • Android
  • Offline sync
05

Cyber Awareness Programme

Security Education · Community

Most breaches begin with a person rather than a protocol. This programme is delivered in person to Sri Lankan school students and built around situations they actually meet online, written in language they recognise and structured closer to a game than a compliance module. Students work through each scenario on the machines in front of them and defend their answers out loud.

  • Browser based scenario quiz of fifteen situational questions
  • Narrated walkthrough of an attack unfolding one step at a time
  • Threat guide covering five social platforms and the specific risk on each
  • Delivered live in school computer laboratories with open discussion
  • Security awareness
  • Curriculum design
  • JavaScript
  • Workshop delivery
